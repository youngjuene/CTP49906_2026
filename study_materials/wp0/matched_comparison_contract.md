# Matched comparison contract

**Contract ID:** `matched-comparison/1.0.0`
**Structural status:** `PASS`
**Concrete fidelity/interface freeze:** `BLOCKED`

This contract is frozen before treatment-UI composition so comparability is not
reconstructed after exposure. It freezes structure, dependency direction, and the
fields that must match. It does not fabricate unresolved values or approvals.

## Neutral production kernel

Both arms use the same source/trim/order/mix/accessibility/assistance manifest,
V1/V2 registration, and private/export schemas. WP-0 does not authorize building a
Marimo nonlinear editor. Broader tools are permitted only after the common outcome
or when allocation, assistance, and editor choice are matched and recorded.

## Contamination boundary

The comparison surface must not import treatment modules. Before common
post-measures it must not render `logit lens`, `layer probe`, `attention knockout`,
or `internal ablation`. Student portfolios and student-facing UI do not contain
condition assignment. Treatment and comparison never import one another; both
depend inward on `curriculum_common/`. Any post-measure debrief is separately
configured and recorded.

## Fidelity and deviation contract

Every matched dimension must acquire an owner, value, tolerance, and approval
status. Numeric tolerances remain `null` until pilot evidence. Both arms append the
same timing, help, tools, source deviation, accessibility support, disruption, and
completion fields. Deviations are append-only and cannot be silently repaired.
Critical-deviation/exclusion handling must be prespecified by the human methods
owner. A unilateral treatment change invalidates the freeze until comparison and
fidelity review are renewed.

The following study values remain explicitly `null`: allocation unit, method, and
concealment; RQ1 estimand; sample size/power; missingness; multiplicity; fidelity
thresholds; and rater reliability/adjudication rules.

## Stop decision

The mirrored structural contract passes. Concrete treatment-interface freeze is
blocked until time/help/source/editor/export/instrument/fidelity values, cognitive-
demand rationale, allocation/estimand decisions, and accountable approvals exist.

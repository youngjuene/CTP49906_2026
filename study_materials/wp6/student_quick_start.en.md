# Student quick-start

**Document ID:** `student-quick-start/1.0.0`

1. Choose **Saved course replay** unless your instructor has prepared the live
   GPU route. Saved replay is a teaching example, not your research evidence.
2. Complete the notebook in heading order: orient, plan, register V1, observe,
   experiment once, compare readings, revise V2, and export.
3. Before every model run, commit a prediction. After it, record observation,
   interpretation, limitation, a rival explanation, and the next control.
4. When sharing with an audience, download only the validated blinded packet.
   It excludes creator intention, machine output, condition, private IDs, and
   content hashes.
5. Audience respondents use the separate no-GPU surface. Each person validates
   the packet, completes the form independently, and downloads one reading JSON.
6. Import two readings with distinct audience and session pseudonyms. Creator
   and machine readings stay hidden until both imports validate.
7. Compare the readings without ranking a human or machine answer as the truth.
   Name a reading that the available sources do not represent.
8. Download your private portfolio only when its validation status is valid.

## Data boundary

Uploads and records are processed in the notebook's hosted session/container,
not solely on your device. Nothing automatically sends your artifact, process
record, or audience response to an instructor. A download happens only when you
press its control. Ask the instructor about the platform's retention policy.

Captions, transcripts, and descriptions are human-correctable access supports.
They never silently become model inputs. Use a text alternative or downloadable
data table whenever a chart is not usable for you.

This classroom route is not research-ready and participation in class activity
is not research consent.

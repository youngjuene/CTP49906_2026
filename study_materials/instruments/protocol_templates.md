# Teaching rehearsal and protocol templates

**Template version:** `common-classroom-protocol-templates/0.1.0-pilot`  
**Research status:** disabled pending institutional and accountable-human approval

## Matched teaching rehearsal

- route ID and immutable candidate commit/tag:
- facilitator pseudonym and contact policy version:
- approved brief/source/editor/export identifiers:
- hardware and accessibility supports available:
- scheduled and observed stage times:
- V1, audience exchange, V2, and common outcome completion:
- help, tool, source, accessibility, disruption, and completion deviations:
- reset/delete/private-download checks:
- explicit statement that two audience readings are the classroom minimum rather
  than a research sample-size justification:

Any unilateral change is a deviation until both surfaces and the accountable
fidelity reviewers renew the comparison. Null tolerances remain null until pilot
evidence and methods approval exist.

## Accessibility and localization review

- keyboard-only route completed without pointer-only actions:
- focus order and visible focus checked in rendered Marimo:
- labels, headings, status messages, and errors checked in the accessibility tree:
- caption/transcript and visual description edited by a human and rechecked:
- no overlay silently used as a computational input:
- English/Korean key inventory equal:
- qualified semantic, terminology, and line-expansion review complete:
- accommodation or timing adjustment recorded as support, not student failure:

Static checks do not establish WCAG conformance or translation quality. Attach the
human/browser evidence separately.

## Privacy and Teaching-mode closeout

- Teaching was the initial and fail-safe mode:
- no automatic student-data egress observed:
- any audience packet had explicit sharing permission and the frozen allowlist:
- private download was user initiated:
- no student-facing allocation field entered UI, packet, or portfolio:
- temporary uploads and session state reset on shared devices:
- Molab/container retention and incident questions escalated to the accountable
  institutional owner:

## Unresolved Research-mode gate

Do not enable Research mode until a compatible approved configuration names the
protocol and wording versions, independent permission scopes, permitted fields,
destination, retention period, withdrawal path, contact, expiry, schema, course
release, instrument/rubric versions, rater plan, and institutional determination.
An in-notebook acknowledgement is not consent.

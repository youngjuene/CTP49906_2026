## Decision-specific guidance

Guidance describes what must be reviewed; it is not a decision and must not be copied as approval evidence.

### B-R01 — IRB/equivalent

- Required authority: IRB or equivalent authorized research-ethics body
- Required scope: Approve, exempt, or otherwise issue the required formal determination for the proposed research.
- Required evidence:
  - Research protocol and supporting materials submitted to the proper body
  - Formal IRB/equivalent determination
  - Record reference or signed decision
- Default revisit triggers:
  - Material change to the approved scope, materials, systems, population, or procedure
  - New legal, ethical, institutional, licensing, accessibility, privacy, security, or safety requirement
  - Evidence expires, is withdrawn, is superseded, or no longer matches the implemented state
  - Incident, complaint, adverse event, audit finding, or previously unknown risk relevant to this decision
  - Any protocol amendment or departure affecting the research determination or analysis
- Legacy governance IDs: GOV-01
